## The problem

At BearStudio, parking spots are limited and the office sits on the outskirts of town. People were relying on a Slack channel to coordinate carpools every morning, a chaotic, easily-missed system that nobody loved.

We needed something purpose-built: a simple way to say "I'm driving tomorrow, who wants a ride?" without the noise.

## What Cowat does

Cowat is a carpooling app designed around everyday commutes, not one-off trips. Drivers publish their upcoming rides, passengers request a seat, and the app handles the rest: matching, notifications, and scheduling.

The focus is on **making it feel human**. It's not about optimizing routes or saving milliseconds. It's about connecting people who share the same roads, building trust, and making the daily commute a little less lonely.

## My role

I'm the lead developer on Cowat v2, a full ground-up rebuild of the original app. I kept the core features that worked, redesigned the entire UI, and added a layer of UX improvements that the first version was missing.

I continue to ship new features and maintain the project day-to-day.

## Multi-tenant & open to others

Cowat is in production and used daily at BearStudio. But it's built as a multi-tenant app from the start. We're open to setting it up for other teams or companies that face the same parking & commute challenges.
